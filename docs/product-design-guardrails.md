# Crayons Product Design Guardrails

## Locked product character

### Crayons Bridge — Business Class
Crayons Bridge is the business-class product in the Crayons ecosystem.

Design consequences:
- Executive, premium, restrained, precise, trustworthy.
- Calm information hierarchy and generous but controlled spacing.
- Canonical Bridge logo presented cleanly without distortion, clipping, theatrical animation or entertainment-style effects.
- Motion is minimal and purposeful: navigation, hover, focus and state transitions only.
- No decorative sound.
- UI language must reflect persisted business state and the Bridge authority model.
- Avoid duplicate workflows, demo/fabricated claims, decorative operational dashboards and consumer-streaming visual patterns.

### Crayons Loop — General Public Entertainment
Crayons Loop is the consumer entertainment product.

Design consequences:
- Cinematic, expressive and visually alive.
- Motion, logo animation and branded audiovisual moments are allowed when they improve the viewer experience.
- Sound must never be loud/unexpected; respect browser autoplay limits and require or follow user interaction where needed.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and accessible controls.
- Consumer presentation must remain grounded in Bridge-authorized catalog, rights and entitlement state.

## Ecosystem boundary
Bridge controls ingest, preparation, QC, legal, rights, licensing, authorized delivery and business/revenue operations. Loop serves the authorized audience and owns consumer merchandising, profiles, checkout/entitlements, playback and viewing state.

These guardrails are product-direction constraints, not proof of production readiness. Exact-head CI, matching deployment evidence and runtime verification remain release gates.
