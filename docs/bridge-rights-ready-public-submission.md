# Crayons Bridge public rights-ready submission field map\n\n## Product rule\n\nPublic onboarding is now a rights-holder title intake for **Independent Creator** and **Studio / Company** accounts. **Buyer / Acquisitions** accounts enter the buyer workspace and do not submit rights packages.\n\n**Crayons Bridge** remains the media supply chain, rights and licensing control plane. Legal owner wording remains **StreamVista OPC Pvt Ltd.** and is shown in legal/footer context, not as the product identity.\n\nThe public form creates one canonical Bridge title and structured draft records for rights, legal review and buyer packaging. It never grants **LICENSING_READY**.\n\n## Field map\n\n| Group | Field | Required | Control | Stored in | Licensing Ready relevance |\n|---|---|---:|---|---|---|\n| Account | Your name | Yes | Single bridge_destination_packages.consumer_metadata | Required package scope |\n\n## Evidence handling\n\nDo **not** ask for public URLs, contract numbers, or long rights descriptions.\n\nAfter submission, the title workspace must collect evidence through Bridge's authenticated asset/document flow:\n\n- authorization / chain-of-title evidence\n- contributor, music and artwork rights where applicable\n- classification / censor evidence\n- private screener\n- approved master\n- artwork required by the destination package\n- subtitle / audio / accessibility assets as applicable\n\nEvidence files remain private to Bridge. Loop receives only the approved publication package; it never receives source masters, contracts or legal documents.\n\n## Licensing Ready gate\n\nA title may move from **RIGHTS_REVIEW → LICENSING_READY** only when all minimum conditions are true:\n\n1. Canonical title metadata is complete.\n2. At least one valid rights grant exists with territories, languages, media and a non-expired start/end window.\n3. Rights basis and documentary authorization evidence are present.\n4. Legal case is **APPROVED**.\n5. QC case is **PASSED**.\n6. A verified private screener exists.\n7. A verified master exists.\n8. Required artwork/package inputs exist.\n9. At least one buyer/delivery destination package exists and is still **HOLD/READY**, never silently assumed authorized.\n\n**LICENSING_READY** means the title is commercially licensable; it does not mean buyer-visible. Buyer visibility remains a separate Bridge publication gate.\n\n## UX rules\n\n- No generic rights-description free-text field.\n- No public upload of contracts through arbitrary URL fields.\n- No public buyer-visible screener URL.\n- Use structured controls for territory, language, media, exclusivity, window and sublicensing.\n- Keep legal owner wording as **StreamVista OPC Pvt Ltd.** only where legal ownership is relevant.\n- Product UI says **Crayons Bridge**, not StreamVista Cloud X.
